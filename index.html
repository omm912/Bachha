To My Bachha
❤️ Happy Girlfriend Day, Meri Jaan. 🌹

Kabhi socha nahi tha ki meri zindagi mein koi aisa aayega jo meri har chhoti si khushi ki wajah ban jayega. Tumhare saath bitaya hua har pal mere liye bahut special hai. Chahe hum baatein karein, hasein, ya bas ek dusre ke saath chup baithen, har lamha mere dil ke bahut kareeb hai.

Thank you meri zindagi mein aane ke liye. Thank you mujhe samajhne ke liye, meri har baat sunne ke liye, aur meri life ko itna khoobsurat banane ke liye. Shayad main har baar apni feelings sahi tarah se express nahi kar pata, lekin itna zaroor jaanta hoon ki tum meri duniya ka sabse pyara hissa ho.

Main promise karta hoon ki har mushkil waqt mein tumhare saath khada rahunga. Tumhari smile hamesha meri priority rahegi, kyunki tumhari khushi hi meri sabse badi khushi hai.

Bas ek hi dua hai ki hum dono ka saath hamesha aise hi bana rahe. Har naya din tumhare saath aur bhi khoobsurat ho, aur har yaad humein aur kareeb le aaye.

Happy Girlfriend Day, Bachha. ❤️
❤️ I love you more than words can ever explain. ❤️
Aaj, kal aur hamesha... sirf tum. 🌹❤️
